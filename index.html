About Barrington Ridge

Barrington Ridge is a Michigan‑based property leasing consultancy that partners with homeowners to unlock the full potential of their residences. We handle everything from preparing and promoting your property to managing occupancy and maintenance, transforming your home into a premium, revenue‑generating experience while respecting your personal aesthetic.

From suburban homes to downtown lofts across Michigan, we curate a portfolio of distinctive residences designed for unique, high‑end experiences. Our tailored leasing solutions provide owners with reliable income and peace of mind. Whether you are away or seeking professional management, we oversee every detail so your property is maintained to the highest standards.

Our Services

Eligibility Evaluation

Property Promotion

Our marketing team crafts distinguished property profiles and leverages premier channels to ensure your residence reaches discerning guests, maximizing your rental potential.

We conduct rigorous screening and verification to ensure every guest meets our high standards and treats your property with care.
